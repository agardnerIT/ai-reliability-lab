# Foundation

Before diving into the tutorials, there are a few concepts worth understanding. You don't need to be an expert, but these ideas will keep coming up, and a basic mental model will make everything click faster.

## What's covered here

- **[Tokens](tokens.md)**: the unit everything in AI is measured and priced in
- **[What's in a Request](anatomy-of-a-request.md)**: why one sentence can cost 1,000 tokens
- **[OpenTelemetry](opentelemetry.md)**: the open standard we use to collect observability data
- **[AWS Bedrock](aws-bedrock.md)**: the AI platform our examples run on
- **[Dynatrace Setup](dynatrace-setup.md)**: getting your credentials ready
- **[Environment Setup](environment-setup.md)**: getting the tutorial code running on your machine (or in your browser)

## How long will this take?

About 15–20 minutes to read everything. You can also skim and come back when something doesn't make sense in a tutorial.

!!! note "Complete beginner?"
    Start with Tokens, then OpenTelemetry. Those two are the most important for understanding the tutorials.
