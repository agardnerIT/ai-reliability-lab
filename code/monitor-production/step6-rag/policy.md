# Customer Support Policy

## Refunds
Full refund available within 30 days of purchase for any reason, no questions asked. Damaged or incorrect items qualify for a full refund at any time with no return required. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to the original payment method. Customers who have waited more than 5 business days for a refund to appear should be advised to contact their bank, and issued a refund confirmation reference number.

## Replacements
Incorrect or damaged items can be replaced free of charge with express shipping at no cost to the customer. The customer keeps the incorrect item if return shipping cost exceeds the item value. Replacements are dispatched within 1 business day and tracked. The customer should receive a tracking number by email within 2 hours of the replacement being raised.

## Compensation
For a first damaged or late delivery: issue a 10% discount code on their next order. For a second or repeat issue with the same customer: issue a 20% discount code plus free express shipping on their next order. For delays over 7 days beyond the estimated delivery date: apply $10 store credit automatically and notify the customer by email. Compensation codes are valid for 90 days and apply to any order over $20.

## Escalation
Any complaint mentioning legal action, solicitors, bank disputes, chargebacks, or social media threats must be escalated immediately to a senior agent — do not draft a response. Any customer who has contacted support more than twice in 30 days should be flagged for escalation review. Escalated cases must be acknowledged within 1 hour and resolved within 24 hours. The escalating agent must log the reason and attach all prior correspondence.

## Tone and Communication
Always apologise sincerely and specifically — never use phrases like "sorry for any inconvenience". Use the customer's first name throughout. Be specific about what action is being taken and when. Never promise a timeframe you cannot guarantee. Responses should be warm, professional, and free of jargon. For complaints involving repeat failures, acknowledge the pattern explicitly rather than treating it as an isolated incident.

## Digital Products and Downloads
Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded or activated, unless the product is proven defective or materially different from its description. Defective digital products are replaced with a working version within 24 hours. If a replacement cannot be provided, a full refund is issued regardless of download status. Customers locked out of digital accounts due to our error receive a 48-hour response SLA and priority support.

## International Orders
International customers are subject to the same refund and replacement policy as domestic customers. Return shipping for international orders is covered by the company for damaged or incorrect items. Customs and import duties paid by the customer are non-refundable by us but the customer may reclaim them via their local customs authority. Delivery estimates for international orders are indicative only and delays beyond 14 days from dispatch should trigger proactive outreach.

## Subscription Cancellations
Customers may cancel a subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — no partial refunds for unused days unless the customer was charged in error. Customers charged after a cancellation request was submitted receive a full refund of the erroneous charge within 2 business days. Pausing a subscription is available for up to 3 months; the agent should offer this before processing a cancellation.

## Lost or Stolen Packages
A package is considered lost if it has not been delivered within 5 business days of the expected delivery date and tracking shows no movement. Lost packages are replaced or refunded at the customer's choice, no police report required for orders under $200. For orders over $200, a signed declaration from the customer is required before replacement or refund. Stolen packages reported with a police reference number are treated identically to lost packages.

## Warranty Claims
All physical products carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty claims require a description of the fault and a photograph where the defect is visible. Approved warranty claims result in a free replacement dispatched within 2 business days. Warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear. For products over 12 months old, a goodwill replacement may be offered at the agent's discretion for long-standing customers.

## Business and Bulk Orders
Business customers with orders over $500 are entitled to a dedicated account manager for complaint resolution. Bulk orders (10 or more units) with defects affecting more than 20% of units qualify for a full batch replacement at no charge. Business customers do not receive discount codes as compensation — instead, account credit is applied directly to their account. SLA for business complaint resolution is 4 business hours, not the standard 24.

## Delivery Disputes
If a customer reports non-delivery but tracking shows delivered, the agent must first ask the customer to check with neighbours and in safe-drop locations. If the package is still not found after 48 hours, treat it as a lost package and follow that policy. Courier errors confirmed by the courier's own investigation result in a full replacement or refund within 1 business day of confirmation. Customers should not be asked to pursue the courier directly — we handle that on their behalf.
