# Customer Support Policy

## Refunds
- Full refund available within 30 days of purchase for any reason.
- Damaged or incorrect items qualify for full refund at any time, no return required.
- Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to the original payment method.

## Replacements
- Incorrect or damaged items can be replaced free of charge with express shipping.
- Customer keeps the incorrect item if return shipping cost exceeds item value.

## Compensation
- First damaged/late delivery: 10% discount code on next order.
- Second or repeat issue with same customer: 20% discount code + free express shipping on next order.
- Delays over 7 days beyond estimated delivery: $10 store credit applied automatically.

## Escalation
- Any complaint mentioning legal action, bank disputes, or social media threats must be escalated to a senior agent — do not draft a response.
- Any customer who has contacted support more than twice in 30 days should be escalated.

## Tone
- Always apologise sincerely, use the customer's first name, and be specific about what action is being taken.
- Never promise a timeframe you cannot guarantee.
